The \texttt{booktabs} package provides commands for clean horizontal rules.
Avoid vertical rules in most publication-style tables.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Common commands for organizing a document.}
  \label{tab:commands}
  \begin{tabular}{ll}
    \toprule
    Command & Purpose \\
    \midrule
    \texttt{\textbackslash section} & Starts a numbered section \\
    \texttt{\textbackslash label} & Marks a location for referencing \\
    \texttt{\textbackslash ref} & Prints the number of a labeled item \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Table~\ref{tab:commands} demonstrates a caption and a cross-reference.
