\section{Product rule}
For $h(x)=x^2 e^x$, the product rule gives
\begin{align*}
  h'(x)
    &= \frac{d}{dx}(x^2)e^x + x^2\frac{d}{dx}(e^x) \\
    &= 2x e^x + x^2 e^x \\
    &= e^x(x^2+2x).
\end{align*}

\section{Chain rule}
For $q(x)=\sin(x^2)$, take the derivative of the outer function and multiply
by the derivative of the inner function:
\[
  q'(x) = \cos(x^2)\cdot 2x = 2x\cos(x^2).
\]

\section{Tangent line}
The tangent line to $q(x)=x^2$ at $x=1$ has slope $q'(1)=2$ and passes through
$(1,1)$. Its equation is
\[
  y-1=2(x-1), \qquad\text{or}\qquad y=2x-1.
\]
